With a background in sociology, art, and creative technology, I am interested in the effect materiality has on our relationships with machines. Currently, I am recontextualizing the robot body as an ephemeral, edible object through my Marshmallow Robots and exploring how food materials can be utilized to create culturally charged, computational interactions.
