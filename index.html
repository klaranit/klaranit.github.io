About Me

I am curious about the influence of materiality in technology. Analog and digital objects alike rely on their visual, physical, and social design for intuitive interaction. But with the rapid increase in automated, agentic, and responsive objects — how do these markers of interaction change? My work explores unconventional, overlooked, and unfamiliar presentations of objects to prod at these boundaries.

Robots & AI · 3D Printing

Robot Fashion

Collaboration with HIRO Group, CU Boulder

Overview

Experimental 3D printed fashion accessories designed to protect humanoid limbs from damage when falling. Using Blender + GenTact (a Blender extension by Carson Kholbrenner) I created TPU accessories that utilized the material's soft, bouncy properties to protect robot limbs from excessive force.

More Info

After joining the HIRO lab, I worked with PhD student Carson Kholbrenner on his soft robotic skin project, learning Blender and GenTact Toolbox to design skins on the robot's morphology. I explored filaments including Verashore and TPU to expand sensory interactions.

I also experimented with a dual-layer skin using PLA and TPU to mimic how human skin changes color when pressed — a blunted spiked PLA underlayer visible to an external camera as a pressure signal. Final skins used TPU and conductive PLA for embedded tactile sensing, printed on a Prusa XL.

Skills

3D printing, CAD, TPU, multimaterial printing

Topics

HRI, tactile interaction design, fashion

Robots & AI · Installation

BioTelia

With Shalimar Alvarado Cruz, Aaron Neyer, Cambria Klinger, Ayesha Rawal — ATLAS design studio

Overview

Interactive installation using fabric, motion capture, and insect-like robots. Positioning the audience as a pollinator, BioTelia combines robotics, materiality, and performance technology to create an alien environment echoing systems of our own world. I designed the robots, which acted as secondary pollinators — each with different interaction properties provoking distinct reactions from the audience.

Skills

Motion capture, interaction design, object characterization

Topics

Human-Robot-Interaction

Handwriting · Typography

Code of Conduct

CU Boulder

Overview

Typeface created from 2000+ handwriting samples collected across CU Boulder whiteboards. Over three weeks I visited campus whiteboards across the library, classrooms, and communal study spaces in disciplines ranging from engineering to art. Letters were isolated using MAXQDA, digitized in Photoshop, and assembled into a typeface in Glyphs Mini. The type specimen re-imagines public school signage in the voice of CU students.

Skills

Data collection, digital typography. Tools: Adobe, Glyphs, MAXQDA

Topics

Handwriting, community spaces, data visualization

Robots & AI · Education

AI Puppet

ACME Lab — with Krithik Ranjan and Suibi Che-Chuan Weng

Overview

Puppet to educate children on AI literacy through a personalized agent. Demonstrated at ATLAS Open Labs — participants described personality attributes and speech restrictions, drew their puppet on a sticky note, then verbally conversed with their constructed AI agent. In progress; positioned to collaborate with local Boulder schools to teach about AI limitations, hallucinations, and capabilities.

Skills

Qualitative analysis, coding

Topics

AI, human-AI interaction, educational tools
